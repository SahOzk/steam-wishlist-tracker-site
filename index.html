01 / PROJECT

The idea behind SWT

Developed around one personal library, not a storefront. Purchases remain on the original store websites.

01 —

Wishlist & price history

Bring wishlist entries, ownership information and USD price comparisons into a single view, with historical context where data is available.

02 —

Bundles with context

See which bundle games are already owned and which are on the wishlist. A more visual, card-based bundle experience is planned.

03 —

Media on the home server

Keep game screenshots and trailers locally, with a unified gallery. Media processing is being adapted to avoid unnecessary AV1 re-encoding.

02 / INTERFACE

A look at the current build
